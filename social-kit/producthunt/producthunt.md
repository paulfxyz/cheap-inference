## Maker's first comment

Hi Product Hunt, Paul here.

I built cheap-inference because most of what I was paying a model for was not my question. It was everything riding along with it: guides loaded in every session, a knowledge base opened for questions that never needed it, files re-read because nothing remembered they were done.

What is inside:
- 13 Python tools that cost 0 tokens to run: a prompt linter, a budget checker, a usage report, a log compactor, a router, a cache simulator and more. Standard library only.
- 42 copy-paste prompts and 100 tips, each cheap to try and easy to undo.
- A 14-chapter field guide and 10 recipes, written to teach.
- Receipts: every bold claim has a command that checks it.

A good first minute: run the budget checker on your own guide and see what it costs you per month in loading alone.

What it does not do: it does not promise a percentage saving, it ships no real prices (you fill in your own), and the linter checks that quotes exist in their sources, not that the sources are true.

It is MIT licensed and free. I would love to hear which tip saved you the most, and which tool you wish existed next.
