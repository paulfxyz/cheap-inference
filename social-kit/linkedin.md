## Main post

Your AI bill is not your question.

Most of what you pay a model for rides along with it: the guide loaded in every session, the knowledge base opened for a question that never needed it, the 500 files re-read because nothing remembered they were done, and the premium model used to rename a file.

So I built a toolkit to attack that waste, and I have just open-sourced version 2.0.0.

cheap-inference is:
- 13 Python tools that spend 0 tokens running (standard library only, no dependencies)
- 42 copy-paste prompts
- 100 small tips, each cheap to try and easy to undo
- 14 chapters and 10 recipes, written to teach

The idea fits on one line: if code can do it, never pay a model to do it.

A few things it does:
- Catches a false quote in a model-written wiki with a script, in milliseconds
- Hashes every source, so a nightly job costs nothing when nothing changed
- Shows why one timestamp at the top of a prompt can zero your cache hit rate
- Climbs a cheap-first ladder one rung at a time, and only with a reason

It also says plainly what it does not do: no promised percentage, no invented prices, and a linter that checks quotes, not truth.
